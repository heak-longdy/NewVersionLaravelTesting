# Implementation Plan: Asset Ownership, Registration Card & Vignette Control Report

Enhance the Core Banking System (Tonle) to capture physical vehicle registration card custody ("កាតគ្រី"), roadworthiness inspection schedules (ឆែកឆេរ), road tax vignette (មីញ៉ែត) renewal windows, and physical filing references, and implement a dedicated multi-tab reporting engine to generate the **Asset Ownership, Registration Card & Mignette Control Report**.

---

## Summary

### Overview
- **Report Name:** Asset Ownership, Registration Card & Vignette/Mignette Control Report (`1_Report Asset Ownership_ Registration Card & Mignette Control Report.xlsx`).
- **Main Business Purpose:** To systematically monitor, control, and audit the physical custody of vehicle registration cards ("កាតគ្រី"), vehicle roadworthiness technical inspection cycles (ការឆែកឆេរលក្ខណៈបច្ចេកទេសយានយន្ត), annual road tax vignette (មីញ៉ែត) renewal windows, archive box/filing references, and legal ownership entities (*TLKH*, *Devco*, *Customer*). It provides an operational and risk management framework across active lease portfolios, repossessed vehicle yards, resold assets, and master vehicle registration logs across 5 specialized reporting sheets (**Summary**, **Current Customer List**, **Repo**, **Resold**, and **Car registration Name**).

### Data Sources
The report consolidates data across **8 source tables** in the Tonle Core Banking System (`tonle1` database):
1. **`MKT_LOAN_CONTRACT`**: Primary active loan contract table supplying contract IDs, customer IDs, officer IDs, branch codes, loan terms, vehicle asset specifications (brand, model, series, chassis VIN, engine number, plate number, color, engine size, year model), delivery dates, and custom registration/custody fields.
2. **`MKT_CUSTOMER`**: Customer master table supplying borrower identity details (full English customer name: `LastNameEn` + `FirstNameEn`, contact details).
3. **`MKT_OFFICER`**: Loan and sales officer table supplying lending officer names (`LastName` + `FirstName`).
4. **`MKT_COLLATERAL_DE`**: Collateral detail table providing document custody records (`ReceivedBy`, `WithdrawalBy`, `WithdrawalDate`), vehicle technical specs, registration plate, issuance date, maturity, and ownership name.
5. **`MKT_LOAN_COLLATERAL`**: Collateral bridge table linking loan contracts (`MKT_LOAN_CONTRACT`) to their corresponding collateral records (`MKT_COLLATERAL_DE`).
6. **`MKT_ASSET_DELIVERY`**: Leased asset delivery table providing physical delivery dates, asset classification (`V` for Vehicle, `E` for Equipment), asset description, and handover notes.
7. **`MKT_FORECLOSED_PROPERTY`**: Foreclosed and repossessed property table supplying vehicle inventory yard locations, physical card locations, vignette handover status, inspection status, and archive file numbers for the *Repo* and *Resold* sheets.
8. **`MKT_CLOSED_LOAN`**: Historical closed/paid-off loan contracts table used to maintain custody trails and historical vehicle ownership records in the *Car registration Name* sheet.

### Schema Updates
To support the operational data capture requirements of this report, **2 core tables** require schema extensions (along with their corresponding `_INAU` in-authorization and `_HIST` history audit twin tables, totaling 6 physical database tables):

1. **`MKT_LOAN_CONTRACT`** (including `MKT_LOAN_CONTRACT_INAU` and `MKT_LOAN_CONTRACT_HIST`):
   - `CodeFiling` (`VARCHAR(35)` / `db.String(35)`): Physical archive box / filing sequence number (e.g., `806`).
   - `Seats` (`INTEGER` / `db.Integer`): Passenger seat capacity (e.g., `5`, `7`, `14`).
   - `CollateralKeep` (`VARCHAR(50)` / `db.String(50)`): Physical document custody status / custodian location (*In TLF*, *In BSP*, *With PU*, *Paid off*, *Sold repossessed*, *Officer Custodian*, *Other*).
   - `CollateralKeepRemark` (`TEXT` / `db.Text`): Explanatory custody notes when custody status is *Other* or remarks.
   - `WithdrawDate` (`VARCHAR(10)` / `db.String(10)`): Date registration card was released or withdrawn (`YYYY-MM-DD`).
   - `RegistrationOwnership` (`VARCHAR(50)` / `db.String(50)`): Ownership entity printed on the registration card (*TLKH*, *Devco*, *Customer*, *Other*).
   - `RegIssueDate` (`VARCHAR(10)` / `db.String(10)`): Vehicle registration card issuance date (`YYYY-MM-DD`).
   - `InspectionDate` (`VARCHAR(10)` / `db.String(10)`): Vehicle roadworthiness inspection date (កាលបរិច្ឆេទឆែកឆេរ, `YYYY-MM-DD`).
   - `InspectionExpiryDate` (`VARCHAR(10)` / `db.String(10)`): Roadworthiness certificate expiry date (`YYYY-MM-DD`).
   - `VignetteRenewStart` (`VARCHAR(10)` / `db.String(10)`): Road tax vignette renewal window opening date (`YYYY-MM-DD`).
   - `VignetteRenewEnd` (`VARCHAR(10)` / `db.String(10)`): Road tax vignette renewal window deadline date (`YYYY-MM-DD`).

2. **`MKT_FORECLOSED_PROPERTY`** (including `MKT_FORECLOSED_PROPERTY_INAU` and `MKT_FORECLOSED_PROPERTY_HIST`):
   - `AssetLocation` (`VARCHAR(100)` / `db.String(100)`): Physical storage/yard location of repossessed asset (*At Devco*, *At yard*, *At customer*).
   - `CardLocation` (`VARCHAR(100)` / `db.String(100)`): Physical registration card location (*Card Kry at Devco*, *Card Kry at Customer*).
   - `VignetteStatus` (`VARCHAR(50)` / `db.String(50)`): Vignette / road tax paper handover status (e.g., `2010`, `2012`, `N/A`).
   - `InspectionStatus` (`VARCHAR(50)` / `db.String(50)`): Roadworthiness inspection certificate status upon repossession/sale.
   - `FileNumber` (`VARCHAR(35)` / `db.String(35)`): Archive file reference number for foreclosed property records.
